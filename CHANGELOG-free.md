# PreGlide (free version)

## v1.1.1 (2026-11-14)

*   Fixed a crash when undoing word learning on older Android versions
*   Removed malformed words from Russian suggestions
*   Problem reports are now shared as ZIP attachments

## v1.1.0 (2026-11-13)

*   Search, rename, and merge learned words, with undo
*   Choose how many times you type a new word before the keyboard learns it
*   Save pasted words by choosing them in the suggestion bar
*   Remove unwanted suggestions even after typing the whole word
*   Improved Russian suggestions for е and ё and for particles after a hyphen
*   Better suggestions for capitalized words after tapping Shift
*   Autocorrect an extra capital letter at the start of a word
*   Caps Lock turns off when switching languages
*   Delete hyphenated words one part at a time with a Backspace swipe
*   Optionally insert a period by pressing Space twice
*   Look up and try keyboard gestures in Help
*   Fixed hard-to-read labels in the light keyboard theme when system dark mode is on

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-31)

*   First public release
*   Russian and English layouts
*   Glide typing
*   Autocorrection
*   Offline AI predictions
*   Emojis with search
*   Clipboard history with screenshots
*   Customizable themes, colors, fonts, sizes, layouts and gestures
